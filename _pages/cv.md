* **UCLA, LCLS subgroup** - PhD Researcher, 2023-present. Investigated nonlinear optics and QED for X-ray laser applications using machine learning, inverse design, and four-wave mixing in gas-filled hollow-core fibers.

* **Ansys / Lumerical** - R&D Research Intern, 2021. Developed automated workflows and machine-learning-supported compact models for physical simulations of MOSFETs and RF-SQUIDs.
