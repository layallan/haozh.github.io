About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UCLA and lead the LCLS subgroup. I work closely with the Laser Science Group at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory. My work combines physics-guided machine learning, inverse design, nonlinear optics, and programmable laser shaping.
