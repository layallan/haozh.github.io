I am a postdoctoral researcher in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles. My research brings together AI for science, ultrafast and nonlinear optics, nanophotonics, and accelerator beam physics.

I work with the LCLS subgroup at UCLA and the Laser Science Group at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory. My current focus is using physics-guided machine learning, inverse design, and programmable laser shaping to advance high-brightness electron and X-ray sources.

Research interests
======

* AI for science, physical AI, digital twins, and reinforcement learning
* Ultrafast science, nonlinear optics, and optical waveform design
* Nanophotonics, metasurfaces, plasmonics, and quantum optics
* High-field light-matter interaction and accelerator beam physics

* SPIE Optics and Photonics Scholarship, 2025
